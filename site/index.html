About this tool & your privacy

DNS maps names to internet services. Registration records describe the registered domain. We bring both together, with the source and uncertainty visible.

Lookups go directly to Cloudflare, Google (as a fallback), IANA and public RDAP services. Some registries restrict browser access or publish limited data. Missing information is shown as unavailable.

This tool does not test website uptime, TLS certificates or page safety. Full URLs are reduced to hostnames before lookup; paths and query strings are not sent. Providers receive the queried names and your IP address.
